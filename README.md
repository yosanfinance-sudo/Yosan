# Yosan

A personal finance and expense management mobile app built to give you complete clarity over your daily spending, bank statements, personal loans, and financial habits.

---

## Overview

Managing finances across bank accounts, UPI transfers, personal loans, and work expenses often leads to confusion. Yosan brings everything together into a clean, distraction-free mobile experience.

With automated statement parsing, dedicated tracking for debts and reimbursements, and clear visual summaries, Yosan helps you make sense of your money without tedious manual entry.

---

## Key Features

- **Automated Bank Statement Importer**: Import your bank PDF and Excel statements directly. The app automatically groups multi-line entries, extracts UPI and reference numbers, and mathematically verifies debits and credits.
- **Smart Categorization Rules**: Create custom rules to automatically organize recurring transactions into proper expense, income, and transfer categories.
- **Lending & Borrowing Sets**: Track money lent to friends, borrowed funds, and advances with dedicated progress cards, repayment tracking, and linked transaction history.
- **Company & Official Expenses**: Keep business and personal spending separate with dedicated tools to track official advances, pending claims, and settlements.
- **Clear Financial Reports**: View intuitive breakdowns of your monthly cash flow, category spending distributions, and balance history.
- **Full History & Safe Edits**: Review changes with multi-level undo capabilities and protected transaction records.
- **Secure Cloud Sync**: Keep your data safe with optional multi-device backup while retaining full offline access on your device.
- **Direct App Updates**: Receive new features and version notes directly within the app.
